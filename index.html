What do “second cousin” and “once removed” mean?

The number says how far back you share family. The word “removed” says how many generations apart you are.

First cousinsYou share grandparents. One of your parents is a brother or sister of one of theirs.
Second cousinsYou share great-grandparents. One of your parents is a first cousin of one of theirs.
Third cousinsYou share great-great-grandparents. One of your parents is a second cousin of one of theirs.
Once removedOne generation apart. Your parent’s first cousin is your first cousin once removed. So is your first cousin’s child.
Twice removedTwo generations apart. Your grandparent’s first cousin, or your first cousin’s grandchild.

Our school in Kalleda

In 1996 the family gave its house in Kalleda, our own village, for a school. It opened with 80 children and 10 teachers. It grew into the Rural Development Foundation: four schools and a junior college in Telangana villages, about half of the students girls. Family and friends in California set up the India Rural Development Fund (IRDF) the same year, so people in the US can give with a tax deduction.
